A few facts about the world we live in

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. It is the densest planet in the Solar System and the largest of the four terrestrial planets. Our planet takes about365 days to orbit the Sun and approximately24 hours to rotate once on its axis. From space, Earth appears blue because of its vast oceans and atmosphere.Earth is home to millions of species, including humans, animals, and plants. Protecting our environment, reducing pollution, and using natural resources responsibly are important for keeping our planet healthy for future generations.
